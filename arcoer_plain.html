Arcoer Nexus

Where 14 Elements Shine Like Magic
What it is

A little home for your favourite artists.

Arcoer Nexus is a desk gadget built for K-pop fans. You get fourteen small collectible tokens — one for each member of BTS and CORTIS. Place a token on the device and that member's "element" comes alive: the screen shows their card, the lights glow in their signature colour, and the whole thing lights up like a tiny constellation. It can play music, spin a little record, and even make your official ARMY Bomb lightstick glow along with it. Everything about it — the case, the electronics, and the software — was designed and built from scratch.

How it works

The magic, explained simply.

📺

The screen

A little colour screen shows the whole experience — each member's card, the glowing gem-and-star artwork, and the different modes. It's the face of the device.

Really a 3.5″ colour display the software draws all its artwork onto.
💡

The lights

Strips of tiny lights wrap the device. Each one can be any colour, so every member glows in their own shade. When you connect a token, its lights bloom and settle — just like the real thing.

Really 66 individually-controllable LEDs — each light takes its own colour command.
🎫

The tokens

Each member has a small token you place on the device. The device instantly knows which one it is — like how your phone reads a tap-to-pay card — and wakes up that member's element.

Really NFC tags (the tap-to-pay technology) read by a sensor, one tiny chip per token.
🔊

The sound

It plays music — either streamed wirelessly from your phone, or from a memory card inside. A separate part of the device is dedicated just to sound, so the visuals never stutter while music plays.

Really a Bluetooth speaker chip plus a memory-card player, with its own dedicated processor.
🪄

The lightstick sync

Hold your official ARMY Bomb nearby and it glows in sync with the device — no wires, no pairing menus. The two just talk to each other through the air and stay matched, even if the signal hiccups.

Really a wireless radio link that streams colours to the lightstick and re-connects itself automatically.
🧠

The brains

Instead of one processor doing everything (and getting overwhelmed), the device splits the work across five — one for the screen, one for sound, and helpers for the tokens and the lightstick. They pass messages to each other so everything stays smooth.

Really five microcontrollers sharing the load and talking over small internal connections.
🌐

Connected

The device joins your Wi-Fi, so it can be controlled from a phone or computer and even show its status online — handy for setting up shots when filming it.

Really Wi-Fi with a small web control panel for remote control.
The Tree of Arcoer

Every member is a star.

Inside the device, all fourteen members live on the Tree of Arcoer — a glowing constellation where each member is a gem orbiting a central star. As you place tokens, the gems light up one by one and connect together, until the whole tree is complete. It's the heart of the whole design.

How it was made

Built by hand, from nothing.

The case was designed in 3D and printed at home. The circuit board — the green board all the electronics sit on — was custom-designed and manufactured specially for this project. Every wire, solder joint, and line of software was done in-house. It started as an idea and became a real, working object.
